Research Focus

As a PhD Researcher in Health Econometrics, my research focuses on the application of structural econometrics modeling, causal inference: point and partial identification, robust inference, and broad microeconometrics. I am particularly interested in policy evaluation, with a passion for the theoretical aspects of econometrics and its applications in health, population and development economics.
